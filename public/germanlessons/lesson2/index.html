Lesson 2 · Nouns & articles

Der, die or das?

The three genders, a/an, plurals & the endings that give the answer away

Score 0 / 0
Don't panic! Articles are famous for being tricky, even advanced learners slip up. Treat this lesson like a game: you will learn a few patterns that let you guess right most of the time, and you only memorise the rest.
Step 1

Every noun has a gender

German nouns are always written with a capital letter, and each one is masculine, feminine or neuter. The gender decides the article, the in English:

masculineder
femininedie
neuterdas

der Baum · the tree
die Uhr · the watch / clock
das Buch · the book

Golden rule: learn every new noun together with its article. Not Baum, but der Baum. The article is part of the word. Try colours: der = blue, die = red, das = green.
Link to Lesson 1: remember er / sie / es? They match the gender of the noun.
Der Baum ist groß. Er ist grün. (er = the tree)
Die Uhr ist teuer. Sie ist neu. (sie = the watch)
Das Buch ist dick. Es ist interessant. (es = the book)
So even a German "it" has three versions!
Step 2

"The" vs "a / an"

Definite (the) = something specific you both know. Indefinite (a / an) = something new or general.

thea / an
masculineder Baumein Baum
femininedie Uhreine Uhr
neuterdas Buchein Buch
pluraldie Bücher(no article)

Der Baum ist groß. Das ist ein Baum.The tree is big. That is a tree.

Die Uhr ist teuer. Das ist eine Uhr.The watch is expensive. That is a watch.

Das Buch ist dick. Das ist ein Buch.The book is thick. That is a book.

ist = is, sind = are. One thing → ist. Several things → sind: Die Bücher sind interessant. (The books are interesting.)
Step 3 · plural

In the plural everything is "die"

It doesn't matter if the singular was der, die or das: the plural is always die.

Die Bäume sind grün.The trees are green.

Die Uhren sind teuer.The watches are expensive.

Die Bücher sind interessant.The books are interesting.

Careful: die is both feminine singular and plural. Die Uhr = one watch, die Uhren = several. Look at the verb: ist or sind?

"a" + plural = nothing. There is no plural of ein, you simply drop the article:

Bücher sind heutzutage so teuer.Books are so expensive nowadays. (books in general)

Die Bücher in diesem Semester sind so teuer.The books this semester are so expensive. (these specific books)

Step 4 · the cheat sheet

Endings & groups that give the gender away

You don't have to learn everything by heart. Many nouns follow a pattern:

der · masculine

  • Male people & animals
    der Onkel, der Kollege, der Löwe
  • Male jobs
    der Arzt, der Lehrer
  • Days, months, seasons
    der Montag, der Mai, der Winter
  • -or
    der Motor, der Monitor
  • -ismus
    der Kapitalismus
  • -en (often)
    der Garten

die · feminine

  • Female people & animals
    die Studentin, die Katze
  • -in (female version of a job/person)
    die Ärztin, die Kollegin
  • -heit / -keit
    die Krankheit, die Möglichkeit
  • -ei
    die Bäckerei
  • -ung
    die Zeitung, die Wohnung, die Übung
  • -tion / -ität
    die Information, die Universität

das · neuter

  • -chen / -lein (small & cute)
    das Mädchen, das Häuschen
  • -ment
    das Dokument, das Apartment
  • -um
    das Zentrum, das Museum
  • Verbs used as nouns
    das Lernen, das Essen
The famous one: das Mädchen. A girl is female, but -chen beats everything and makes the word neuter. The same ending turns das Haus into das Häuschen (little house).
From male to female: add -in. der Kollege → die Kollegin, der Student → die Studentin, der Arzt → die Ärztin. The word often gets an umlaut (Arzt → Ärztin).
No 100% rules. These are strong hints, not laws. Words ending in -en are mostly masculine (der Garten) unless they are verbs turned into nouns (das Essen, "eating / the meal"). When in doubt: check a dictionary and learn the article.
Step 5 · two bonus tricks

Verbs as nouns & compound words

1. A verb becomes a noun with das

Write the infinitive with a capital letter and put das in front:

lernen → das LernenDas Lernen ist schwer. = Learning is hard.

essen → das EssenDas Essen ist gut. = The food is good.

2. Compound nouns: the last word decides

German glues words together. The last part gives the article:

der Apfel + der Saft= der Apfelsaftapple juice
der Kaffee + die Tasse= die Kaffeetassecoffee cup
das Wasser + die Flasche= die Wasserflaschewater bottle

How to practise

3 habits that really work

① Always say the noun with its article, out loud: "der Tisch, der Tisch, der Tisch".
② Put colour-coded sticky notes on things at home: blue / red / green.
③ Learn 3 to 5 new nouns a day, not 50. The patterns above will do the rest.
